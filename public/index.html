Risk-Adjusted Player Rankings

Understanding the Stats

Input Parameters
  • λ: Risk aversion weight, the more variance in results, the lower the adjusted pick value. Set to 0 if you don't care.
  • Recency half-life: The higher this value, the higher more recent results matter. Set to 0 if you want all tournaments to count equally.
  • Years included: What years to include in data? Pick values will recalculate dynamically if you choose to exclude certain years.
  • Total N: Total number of players to calculate pick order/rank against. Set to a default of 40 (10 teams). Converts percentiles into more actionable numbers.
Results
  • Player: Player that has been draftd in the tournamnet. Captains are not calculated. If a player has been a captain, only their results as a drafted player are used.
  • Adjusted Pick Value: Value from -1.0 to 1.0 determining if a player has been drafted too early or too late relative to their results. The higher the value, the better their tournament placements relative to their position in the draft order. Remember, this is only trying to claim that they are being drafted in the incorrect position, not that they are an objective good/bad player.
  • N: Number of tournament placements given the years of data included.
  • Average pick percentile: What percentage of the draft, on average, are picked before this player is picked.
  • Estimated pick order: Given the `Total N` parameter, what placement in the draft are they picked, on average.
  • Average rank percentile: On average, what percentage of players/teams placed higher than this person in the tournament. A value of 0 indicates that they win every tournament. A value of 100 indicates they are last place in every tournament.
  • Estimated rank order: Given the `Total N` parameter, what is their average actual placement in the tournament.
