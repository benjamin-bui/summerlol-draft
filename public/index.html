Risk-Adjusted Player Rankings

A mostly for-fun look at participant performance relative to draft order across the Williams College Summer and Winter LoL tournaments.

Adjusted Pick Value: Weighted difference between average pick percentile and rank percentile.

n: Number of tournaments of data.

Avg. Pick%: Average percentage of draft picked before this player is picked.

Est. Pick Order: Based on Total N players in the draft, where in the pick order this person is picked on average.

Avg. Rank%: Average placement as a percentile; 1st place is 0% and last place is 100%

Est. Rank Order: Based on Total N players in the draft, what placement does this player have on average (assuming teams of 5).

Bayesian TrueSkill score, courtesy of Microsoft (or at least what is publically available and trivial to replicate).

TrueSkill: Conservative TrueSkill score μ - σ; intended to deflate players with a small number of games and high uncertainty.

μ: Estimated skill score. Everyone starts at 1000.

σ: Uncertainty in player skill. Everyone starts at μ/3

Note that win probabilities may not properly reflect listed TrueSkill scores. This is particularly common when there are new players with high uncertainty. Win probabilities are calculated based on the estimated skill score, not the conservative skill score.
